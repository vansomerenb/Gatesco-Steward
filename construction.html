Service 03 — Construction

In-House Construction Depth Connected to Operations

In-house construction depth is central to the GSP value proposition: distressed assets often fail because repairs, turns, and capital decisions are disconnected from operations.

Capital execution without third-party handoffs

Unlike firms that outsource critical work, Gatesco runs construction in-house. Make-ready crews, renovation managers, and major capital project leadership are part of the operating company — the same teams that scope, bid, and execute work across Gatesco's Houston portfolio.

On client assignments, construction execution stays aligned with property management and asset management so the field matches the approved business plan. Gatesco Steward Partners is the client-facing arm of Gatesco, Inc.

Standardized unit turns and make-ready scopes

Repetition and velocity across the portfolio — faster vacancy recovery on distressed and REO takeovers.

Full building rehab

Roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, siding, and concrete work under in-house project leadership.

Common area upgrades and curb appeal

Clubhouses, fitness, landscaping, and exterior improvements for marketing, lease-up, and disposition preparation.

Deferred maintenance clearance

Prioritized remediation of life-safety and habitability items — roofs, siding, parking, sidewalks, and systems.

Phasing that minimizes vacancy

Work schedules that minimize vacancy disruption during active lease-up and stabilization.

Capex execution with operational coordination

Approved budget, committed cost, spend-to-date, and remaining exposure tracked with lender reporting.

Execution is everything.

Deploy construction crews on your collateral

PM teams, crews, vendors, and materials already in-market — ready to mobilize on distressed assets without third-party handoffs.
