Our firm

Built for lenders. Backed by Gatesco.

Gatesco Steward Partners extends the reach of Gatesco, Inc.—a vertically integrated Houston multifamily operator of more than 10,000 units—to institutional lenders and special servicers who need that same operating depth on distressed collateral in the metro.

Our mission

To be the operating partner of choice for institutional lenders holding foreclosed and distressed multifamily collateral—delivering transparent stewardship, measurable performance, and outcomes that protect investor capital.

Relationship to Gatesco, Inc.

Gatesco, Inc. is a vertically integrated Houston multifamily operator—not a third-party manager that outsources capital work. With more than 10,000 units under operations in Greater Houston, the parent company runs property management, unit turns, interior renovation, and full building rehabilitation through in-house teams and established construction leadership.

Gatesco Steward Partners is the dedicated subsidiary that brings this platform to institutional lenders, special servicers, and capital partners on Houston collateral. Same operating muscle. Same vertically integrated execution. Reporting and governance calibrated for your investor requirements.

The operating stack

Our lender-facing practice mirrors the breadth Gatesco already executes across its 10,000+ unit Houston portfolio: institutional-grade property management at the foundation, unit turns and interior programs in the middle, and major building rehabilitation when the business plan demands it.
