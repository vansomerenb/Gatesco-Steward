Service 01 — Property Operations

Day-to-Day Execution That Protects Collateral

Day-to-day execution is paired with asset-level oversight so that leasing, maintenance, turns, capex, and lender strategy move together. Execution is everything.

Property operations on a proven Houston platform

Gatesco Steward Partners is the client-facing arm of Gatesco, Inc. Property management is the foundation of our vertically integrated platform — leasing, resident relations, maintenance, compliance, and staffing executed by teams already operating across Greater Houston.

On distressed, REO, and lender-controlled assignments, property operations are not routine third-party management. The mandate is collateral protection under time pressure: mobilize staff, restore controls, triage life-safety issues, and coordinate turns and capex with the same organization that manages the asset day to day.

Staffing and vendor mobilization

Rapid deployment of on-site teams and vetted Houston vendor networks — no third-party handoffs on distressed collateral.

Leasing, compliance, and resident transition

Aggressive lease-up for vacant REO inventory with transparent concession tracking and professional communication during ownership changes.

Maintenance triage and work-order control

Prioritized remediation of life-safety and habitability items with accountable vendor management and backlog reduction.

Unit turns coordinated with make-ready crews

Direct handoff to in-house make-ready crews — same organization, same accountability, faster vacancy recovery.

Capital and rehab coordination

Property managers work alongside Gatesco construction leadership on interior programs and full building rehabilitation.

Collections and cash control

Disciplined collections, expense, vendor, and approval protocols designed for lenders and special servicers.

Execution is everything.

Whether you are onboarding a single distressed asset or an entire lender-controlled portfolio, our team is ready to discuss scope, timing, and reporting requirements.
