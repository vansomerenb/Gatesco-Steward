Service 02 — Asset Management

Strategic Oversight Aligned With Lender Objectives

Business plans, budgets, market positioning, and lender interface — ensuring NOI, capital deployment, and operational execution remain connected. Operate first. Advise second.

Asset-level oversight that protects collateral value

Day-to-day execution is paired with asset-level oversight so that leasing, maintenance, turns, capex, and lender strategy move together. Our asset management team provides strategic oversight of property performance across Gatesco's vertically integrated platform — ensuring property management, unit turns, and rehabilitation initiatives align with each asset's approved business plan.

Whether the objective is stabilization, refinance, or disposition, we maintain clear milestones and reporting tied to servicer and investor requirements. Gatesco Steward Partners is the client-facing arm of Gatesco, Inc.

Business plans and reforecasts

Recovery budgets, leasing strategy, collections plans, and capex planning within the first 30 days of takeover — with ongoing reforecasting based on market conditions.

Actual-to-budget review with variance explanations

Transparent accountability with written explanations of material deviations — not just spreadsheets.

Disposition planning and KPI tracking

Pre-marketing improvements, operational history, and decision-ready updates to position assets for sale or refinance.

Servicer coordination on approvals and milestones

Direct interface with special servicers on approvals, waivers, and milestone reporting.

Insurance, claims, and risk review

Coverage adequacy, claims management, and lender certificate compliance.

Lender interface

Reporting cadence, investor packages, and guidance on next steps aligned to loan documents and servicer requirements.

Execution is everything.

Align strategy with execution

Contact us to discuss how our asset management services can support your distressed asset, receivership, or lender-controlled portfolio.
