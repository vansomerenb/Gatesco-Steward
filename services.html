Integrated operating platform

Four Disciplines. One Operator.

The platform is designed to reduce handoffs: operating decisions, construction execution, asset oversight, and lender reporting remain connected. Gatesco Steward Partners is the client-facing arm of Gatesco, Inc.

01 — Property Operations

Property Management

Day-to-day execution is paired with asset-level oversight so that leasing, maintenance, turns, capex, and lender strategy move together.

  • Staffing and vendor mobilization
  • Leasing, compliance, and resident transition
  • Maintenance triage and work-order control
  • Unit turns coordinated with make-ready crews
  • Capital and rehab coordination

  • Business plans and reforecasts
  • Actual-to-budget review with variance explanations
  • Disposition planning and KPI tracking
  • Servicer coordination on approvals and milestones
  • Insurance, claims, and risk review

03 — Construction

Construction & Renovations

In-house construction depth is central to the GSP value proposition: distressed assets often fail because repairs, turns, and capital decisions are disconnected from operations.

  • Standardized unit turns and make-ready scopes
  • Full building rehab: roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, siding, concrete
  • Common area upgrades and curb appeal
  • Deferred maintenance clearance for life-safety and habitability
  • Phasing that minimizes vacancy and operational disruption
View construction services → 04 — Financial Reporting

Financial Reporting & Lender Controls

Reporting is the lender's window into the asset. GSP formats deliverables around decision-making: what happened, why it happened, what changed, and what needs approval.

  • Monthly operating statements with variance explanations
  • Rent roll, occupancy, exposure, and leasing activity
  • Capex tracking: approved budget, committed cost, spend-to-date
  • Life-safety, insurance, utilities, claims, servicer decisions
