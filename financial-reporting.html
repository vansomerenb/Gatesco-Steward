Service 04 — Financial Reporting

Financial Reporting & Lender Controls

Reporting is the lender's window into the asset. GSP formats deliverables around decision-making: what happened, why it happened, what changed, and what needs approval.

Decision-ready reporting for lenders and special servicers

For special servicers, lenders, and asset managers, reporting is the primary window into asset performance. Our deliverables emphasize accuracy, timeliness, and the operational context required to evaluate results against the business plan.

Custom reporting available — formatted for weekly or monthly lender updates, CMBS servicer requirements, credit committee packages, and investor reporting. Every report answers: what happened, why it happened, what changed, and what needs approval.

Monthly operating statements

Actuals vs. budget with concise variance explanations — not just spreadsheets.

Rent roll & leasing

Occupancy, exposure, traffic, applications, move-ins, and expirations with unit-level detail.

Capex tracking

Approved budget, committed cost, spend-to-date, and remaining exposure.

Risk & approvals

Life-safety, insurance, utilities, claims, and servicer decisions with pending approval log.
